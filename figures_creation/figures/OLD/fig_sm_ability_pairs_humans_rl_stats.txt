STATISTICS: game score and specialization index at the five ability pairs (humans and RL)
====================================================================================================
Conventions (paper_stats.py, identical in every figure script):
  mean +- SEM; game score 2 decimals, specialization index 1 decimal (%).
  Welch's t-tests: the group with the HIGHER mean is written first ('A > B: (A vs. B; ...)') and t is
    reported as |t|, as in the main text; Welch-Satterthwaite df. Signed t values are in the CSV.
  Two-way between-subjects ANOVAs: effect coding, Type III SS, partial eta^2.
  p = .059 / p < .001.
Definitions
  Game score = deliveries in the 180-s test game (humans) / in a 180-s simulated game ('sims') or
    training episode ('fig4') of the MARL agents.
  RL source: hybrid.
  Specialization index = 100 * |(D1 - D2)/(D1 + D2) + (C2 - C1)/(C1 + C2)| / 2 (D deliveries,
    C cuts per player); no deliveries or no cuts -> 0.
    Human cuts: 'start_cutting'. RL cuts: per-agent cut counters of the training data.
  Units: humans = team; RL = training (mean over its episode window), as in the SI pretraining section.
